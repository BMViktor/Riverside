Prices

The price is for the whole house.

The number of guests does not change the accommodation price — only the tourist tax. Up to 7 guests.

What the price covers

  • Sole use of the house and the garden
  • Utilities
  • Bed linen and towels
  • Pool, sauna, kayak, boat, pedalo, bicycles
  • Parking within the property
  • Wi-Fi
  • Cot and high chair
  • Dog, if house-trained

How payment works

  1. You send the booking request, and we answer within 24 hours.
  2. Once we confirm, you transfer the deposit — that is what makes the booking live.
  3. The balance by transfer 14 days before arrival, or in cash when you get here.
  4. On arrival, the tourist tax and the security deposit in cash.
