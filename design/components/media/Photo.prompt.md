Every image in the brand goes through Photo so placeholders are honest and scrims are consistent.

```jsx
<Photo ratio="21 / 9" square scrim caption="A Körös partja a ház alatt" />
<Photo src="/assets/kert.jpg" alt="Kert" ratio="4 / 3" />
```

Use `scrim` whenever text sits on the image; never dim a photo just for style.
